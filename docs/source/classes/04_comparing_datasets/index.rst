.. _class_04:

04 · Comparing datasets
================================================================================

.. note::
   The material of this class is in preparation.

Goal
----

Compare two precipitation datasets of the `FROGS database <https://frogs.ipsl.fr/download-data/>`_ to
illustrate the uncertainty between observations.

- Example: download two datasets, plot maps of their difference and their time series as two curves.

Material available now
----------------------

None yet. :ref:`extra_01` compares one dataset at different zoom levels with the mean absolute error,
the same idea for two versions of one dataset.

Your analysis
-------------

From this class on, you fill a page of this webpage with your plots and analysis. The page template
comes with the material of the class.
