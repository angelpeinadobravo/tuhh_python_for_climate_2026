06 · Working with zoom levels
================================================================================

.. note::
   The material of this class is in preparation.

Goal
----

Explain the HEALPix zoom levels with maps.

Material available now
----------------------

- :ref:`extra_00`: the zoom levels (cells and spacing per zoom) and exercise 2 (maps at zoom 0, 3 and 6).
- :ref:`extra_01`: global means at zoom 0, 3, 6 and 8 and their error; maps of a region at zoom 6 and 8.

Your analysis
-------------

From this class on, you fill a page of this webpage with your plots and analysis: an example page,
``pages/06_zoom_levels/my_page.rst`` in JupyterLab, that you change into yours. It comes with the material of the class.
